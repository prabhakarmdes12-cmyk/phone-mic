Capture the processed, mixed session as lossless 48 kHz mono WAV. Preview after stopping and export through Android’s Share Sheet.

02 / CONNECT

Two-Phone Mode

Keep a speaker station by your Bluetooth speaker while your presenter phone sends audio over trusted local Wi-Fi. Discover stations automatically, with packet reordering and a 30–60 ms jitter buffer.

03 / CUE

Chiti PA Soundboard

Trigger an announcement chime, synthesized applause, or alert siren. Sounds mix into the microphone channel before the permanent peak limiter.

Keep speaker volume low and your microphone away from the speaker. A digital limiter does not eliminate acoustic feedback. Wi-Fi audio is unencrypted: use trusted networks only. Bluetooth adds device-dependent delay.
