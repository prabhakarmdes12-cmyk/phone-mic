Native Architecture

Zero-GC Native C++ Audio Pipeline

Standard Android audio frameworks trigger frequent garbage collection pauses that ruin live speech. PhoneMic operates entirely in native memory.

Google Oboe / AAudio C++ Engine

Direct interface to Android Audio HAL with lock-free single-producer single-consumer (SPSC) ring buffers. Eliminates runtime garbage collection jitter.

Permanent -0.5 dBFS Brickwall Limiter

Guarantees that combined voice, soundboard cues, and continuous background music can never exceed -0.5 dBFS. Protects audience ears and expensive PA drivers.

Two-Phone Wi-Fi Wireless Relay

Overcomes 10-meter Bluetooth range limitations. Phone 1 acts as a wireless presenter mic streaming raw PCM over local Wi-Fi UDP to Phone 2 connected to the stage PA.

Adaptive Feedback Notch Filter

Automated zero-crossing analysis monitors sustained pure-tone howling resonances and automatically engages narrow notch filtering within 30 milliseconds.
