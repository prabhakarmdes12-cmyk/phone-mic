Engineered for Real-World Sound

Standard Android audio drivers suffer from garbage-collection hiccups and feedback loops. PhoneMic replaces the entire audio path with high-performance native code.

Oboe / AAudio C++ Native Engine

Direct low-level Android audio pipelines using lock-free single-producer single-consumer (SPSC) ring buffers. Zero garbage collection pauses on the audio callback thread.

Permanent -0.5 dBFS Safety Limiter

Every audio frame passes through a brickwall safety peak limiter and adaptive notch filter. Protects audience ears and expensive PA speaker drivers from phone drops or sudden howling feedback.

Two-Phone Wi-Fi Wireless Relay

Overcome Bluetooth range limits: stream raw PCM over local Wi-Fi UDP using mDNS auto-discovery. Phone 1 acts as your roaming wireless presenter mic, while Phone 2 is plugged directly into the stage PA.

Singing Reverb & Continuous BGM Soundboard

Sing live over speakers with studio vocal effects (Karaoke plate reverb, slapback echo, chorus) and loop 4 seamless background grooves (Lo-Fi Chill, Pop Acoustic, Ambient Drone, R&B Beat) mixed right into your broadcast.

Install in 3 Simple Steps

Because PhoneMic is an open-source native APK, install directly to your device in 60 seconds.

1

Download the APK

Tap Download APK on your phone. If Android notifies that the file might be from an unknown source, tap Download anyway.

2

Allow Unknown Apps

Tap the downloaded file. When prompted, tap Settings and toggle on Allow from this source for your browser.

3

Pair & Amplify

Tap Install. Open PhoneMic, pair your Bluetooth or AUX speaker, grant audio permissions, and tap the central broadcast button to speak!

Technical Architecture & Build Specifications

Target Operating System Android 8.0 (Oreo, API 26) through Android 14 (API 34)
Architectures (ABIs) ARM64 (v8a), ARM32 (v7a), x86_64
Audio Processing Engine Google Oboe 1.8+ / AAudio C++ Native Engine (with Java AudioRecord fallback)
Buffer Architecture Lock-free single-producer single-consumer (SPSC) ring buffer (48 kHz 16-bit PCM)
Acoustic Safety Permanent -0.5 dBFS Brickwall Limiter + Dynamic Feedback Notch Filter
Network Protocol Local Wi-Fi UDP Streaming (Port 50005) + mDNS/NSD Service Discovery
Accessibility Compliance WCAG 2.1 Level AAA (7:1 contrast, semantic hierarchy, keyboard/screen-reader nav)
