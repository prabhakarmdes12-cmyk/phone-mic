Why we built this

No ads. No subscriptions. No nonsense.

Most microphone apps on the app store are filled with pop-up video ads, hidden weekly charges, and audio delay. We built PhoneMic to be the simple, honest tool we actually wanted to use.

What other mic apps do

  • Loud video ads mid-speech: Nothing is worse than an unexpected casino game ad blasting through your speakers while you're talking.
  • Sneaky weekly charges: Sneaky \$10/week recurring charges hidden behind tricky "Continue" buttons.
  • Dangerous squealing feedback: No volume protection, meaning sudden high-pitched shrieks can hurt ears and damage speakers.
  • Sending audio to the cloud: Closed-source code and background trackers listening to your room.

The PhoneMic promise

  • 100% ad-free, forever: No banners, no popups, no trackers. Your live speech will never be interrupted.
  • Completely free beta: Download and use every feature right now. No paywalls, no trial timers, no account required.
  • Built-in ear safety: A permanent limiter stops feedback squeals and loud clatter before they hit your speakers.
  • Your voice stays on your phone: Audio never touches the internet or an external server. 100% open-source.

Under the hood

Built for speed and zero lag

Standard Android apps stutter because the system periodically pauses to clean memory. PhoneMic runs low-level C++ code directly on your phone's audio chip for instant sound.

Google Oboe Real-Time Engine

Communicates directly with your phone's sound hardware to stream voice with virtually no delay, avoiding Java pauses.

Ear & Speaker Limiter

Capped at -0.5 dBFS so loud coughs, dropped phones, or close-range feedback never damage your speakers or ears.

8-Phone Wi-Fi Hub & Mesh

Need more range than Bluetooth? Mix up to 8 phone mics into 1 speaker, or use instant multicast PTT intercom across your local Wi-Fi without internet or external hardware.

Feedback Protection

Monitors for high-frequency squealing loops and automatically engages notch filtering within 30 milliseconds.
