Oboe / AAudio C++ Native Engine

Direct low-level Android audio pipelines using lock-free single-producer single-consumer (SPSC) ring buffers. Zero garbage collection pauses on the audio callback thread.

Permanent -0.5 dBFS Safety Limiter

Every audio frame passes through a brickwall safety peak limiter. Protects audience ears and expensive PA speaker drivers from phone drops, taps, or sudden howling acoustic feedback.

Zero Auto-Rebroadcast Privacy

The microphone strictly mutes upon Bluetooth disconnection, incoming telephone or VoIP calls, and app minimization. It will never re-broadcast without your explicit tap.

Dynamic Speech Tone Presets

Switch between Natural (transparent reproduction), Clear (80 Hz low-rumble filter + presence boost), and Loud (peak compression for outdoor PA).

Push-to-Talk (PTT) & Touch Lock Mode

Instant walkie-talkie mode: hold the button to broadcast and release to mute instantly. Engage One-Swipe Touch Lock to prevent pocket taps during lectures.

Adaptive Acoustic Feedback Notch Filter

Continuous resonance detection spots microphone-to-speaker feedback oscillations in real time and automatically applies pinpoint notch cuts before squeals can escalate.

How to Install on Your Android Phone

Because PhoneMic is an open-source native tool, you can install the APK directly in 3 easy steps:

1

Download the APK

Tap the Download APK button above on your Android browser. If prompted that the file might be harmful, select Download anyway.

2

Allow Unknown Apps

Tap the downloaded file in your browser's download manager. If Android shows a security warning, tap Settings $\to$ enable Allow from this source.

3

Pair & Amplify

Tap Install. Open PhoneMic, pair your Bluetooth speaker, grant Microphone permissions, and tap the central broadcast button to speak!
